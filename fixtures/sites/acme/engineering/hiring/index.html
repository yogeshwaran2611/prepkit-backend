How we hire engineers

Our process is deliberately short and we publish it so nobody has to guess.

  1. Intro call (30 minutes) with an engineer, not a recruiter. We talk about what you have built and answer your questions about the domain.
  2. Take-home exercise (3 hours, paid). A small scheduling problem drawn from our real domain. We do not use algorithm puzzles.
  3. Take-home review (60 minutes). We read your code together and ask you to extend it live. We care how you reason about trade-offs.
  4. System design round (60 minutes). Design a service that ingests vessel schedules from multiple ports with different data quality. We look for how you handle partial failure and inconsistent inputs.
  5. Team conversation (45 minutes). Mentoring, on-call, disagreement, and how you like to work.

Total elapsed time is usually two weeks. We give a decision within three working days of the last round.

We always ask about a production incident you owned end to end, and about a time you mentored someone through work they found hard.
